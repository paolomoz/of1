of1 — Recording script

Demo script

arco.coffee · ~4 minutes · no audience
01
Open arco.coffee — just browse
~30 sec
Navigate to arco.coffee. Don't click anything yet — just let the homepage sit. Then slowly scroll.
Say this
"This is arco.coffee. A coffee brand — and a generative website. Every element on this page is designed to be personalized. Let me show you what that means."
02
Browse a few pages — build intent
~30 sec
Click into 2–3 pages with a consistent theme — products, a story, a category. Deliberate browsing. Then come back to the homepage.
Say this
"I'm just browsing. But of1 is paying attention — every page I visit, every product I look at. It's building a picture of what I'm here for."
03
Click Personalize — show the transformation
~1 min
Open the of1 side panel. Click Personalize. Let the page rewrite. Don't rush — let the transformation land before you speak.
Say this
"Based on what I just browsed — here's what of1 decided to change. And here's why it changed each one."
04
Walk the widget — before / after / reasoning
~1 min
Click through each mutation in the widget. Toggle before → after for at least two changes. Read the AI reasoning out loud for one of them.
Say this
"This headline was rewritten. This product was swapped. This CTA changed. It's not guessing — it's explaining itself. That's the thing. The AI shows its work."
05
Close — the pitch
~30 sec
Come off the widget. Let the personalized page sit in frame. Speak to camera.
Say this
"This is of1. A website that pays attention — so every visitor finds exactly what they came for, as if it had always been there. We're in early access. of1.live."
