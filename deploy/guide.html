What is of1?
The 30-second brief for demoers
of1 stands for Audience of One. We build AI-powered websites that personalize every page for every visitor.
arco.coffee is a generative website — built from the ground up to showcase what of1 can do. Use it as your primary demo surface.
The extension is a demo tool to experience a taste of of1 on any site.
Setup checklist
Complete before the booth opens — click to check off
Download the extension zip from the shared folder and decompress it to your Desktop
Open chrome://extensions — enable Developer mode (top-right toggle)
Click Load unpacked → select the unzipped extension folder → confirm of1 appears in the toolbar
Test the extension on wknd.site before the demo — open the site, click Activate in the extension, wait ~1 minute for indexing, browse several adventure pages, then return to the homepage and press Personalise
Keep wknd.site as your fallback audience website — it works reliably with the extension
Demo script
Full flow — approximately 8–12 minutes
1
Ask the audience for a website~1 min
Ask someone in the audience to name any website they know — ideally a brand or company site. wknd.site is a reliable fallback if nothing comes up. Navigate to it and open the of1 side panel.
Say this
"Give me any website — a brand you know, a company site. We're going to run of1 on it, live, right now."
Tip
Avoid sites with aggressive bot protection (banks, government). Content-rich sites like retail, media, or B2B SaaS work best.
2
Activate the extension on their site~1 min indexing
Open the of1 side panel, click Activate on the domain. The extension starts crawling — reading the site's structure, indexing pages, building a content model. Watch the page count climb. This takes 1–2 minutes.
Say this
"I just activated of1 on this site. Right now it's indexing most important pages — understanding the structure, the content, the blocks. It's building a model of what this site knows. Give it a minute."
3
Pivot to arco.coffee~3–4 min
While their site indexes, navigate to arco.coffee. This is your showcase — a site built from scratch as a generative website. Walk through it: show the homepage, a product page, the story. Then click Personalize and show the transformation.
Say this
"This is arco.coffee — a coffee brand we built as a generative website. Every element is designed to be personalized. Watch what happens when I click Personalize."
What to highlight
Point to specific mutations in the widget: "This headline was rewritten. This product was swapped. This CTA changed. And here's the reason the AI gave for each one." The reasoning is the reveal.
4
Tease — back to their site~30 sec
End the arco demo by teasing that you're about to do the same on the site they gave you. Build anticipation before switching tabs.
Say this
"That was a site we built for this. Now let's try the same thing on the site you gave me — one we've never touched."
5
Browse the audience's site~2–3 min
Navigate back to the audience's site. Browse deliberately — look at a product, read a page, click into a category. You're building intent signals. Take your time. The extension is reading every click, every scroll, every page you land on.
Say this
"I'm just browsing — the extension is watching. Every page I visit, every product I look at, it's building a picture of what I'm here for. It doesn't track me. It just pays attention."
Intent building
Browse 3–5 pages with a consistent theme (e.g. all outdoor gear, or all enterprise pricing). The more consistent your browsing, the stronger the intent signal and the more dramatic the personalization.
6
Return to homepage — click Personalize~1 min
Navigate back to the homepage. Open the side panel and click Personalize. The page rewrites in real time — headlines shift, content reorders, CTAs change. Use the widget to walk through each mutation.
Say this
"Based on what I browsed, here's what of1 decided to change — and here's why it changed each one."
If nothing changes
The site may not have enough indexable content, or the intent signal was too weak. Refresh, browse 2–3 more pages with a clear theme, then try again. wknd.site is your safe fallback.
7
Navigate the widget — before/after~1 min
Use the mutation widget to walk through each change. Toggle between the original and personalized versions. Show the reasoning for each mutation. Let the audience ask questions here — this is the most engaging moment.
Say this
"This is the original. This is what of1 changed it to. And this is the AI's reasoning. It's not guessing — it's explaining itself."
8
Capture and share the demo~30 sec
Click the Share button in the widget. A QR code appears. The audience can scan it to get their own copy of the demo — a permanent before/after page showing exactly what changed on a real website, live at the event.
Say this
"Scan this. You'll get a page showing exactly what changed, with the AI's reasoning for each mutation. Something to take away from today."
FAQ
Questions you'll get at the booth
Is this tracking me?
No. of1 reads behavioral signals within a single session — what you browse, how long you stay, where you come from. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't build a cross-site profile, and doesn't store personal data. The intelligence stays local.
How is this different from A/B testing?
A/B testing shows different versions of a page to different segments. of1 creates a unique version for each individual visitor, in real time, based on who they are right now — not which bucket they were assigned to last week.
How long does it take to implement?
For a site like arco.coffee, built from scratch as a generative website, we co-build it together. For an existing site, we work alongside your team to define the content model, the personalization rules, and the block library. Timeline depends on site complexity.
Does it work on any website?
The demo extension can run on most content-rich sites. A production generative website is built with of1's architecture from the start — so the full experience requires partnership, not just an extension install.
What AI model does it use?
of1 uses a combination of models depending on the task — fast edge inference for intent detection, and more capable models for content generation. The specific stack is part of the co-innovation conversation.
What does "co-innovate" mean?
We don't sell a product you install and configure. We partner with brands to build their generative website — designing the content model, building the block library, and tuning the personalization logic together. arco.coffee is what that looks like when it's done.
