Privacy Policy

This privacy policy describes how the Audience of One Preview Chrome extension ("the Extension") collects, uses, and protects your data.

What we collect

The Extension collects the following data to provide AI-powered website personalization:

  • Browsing activity — pages you visit on activated domains, including page titles and timestamps, to understand your interests and intent.
  • Page content — text, images, and structure of web pages you visit, used to generate personalized alternatives.
  • Interaction signals — scroll depth, time on page, and navigation patterns to infer preferences.

How we use your data

  • To analyze your browsing behavior and generate personalized content variations.
  • To send page content to our AI service for real-time personalization.
  • To store indexed page data locally and on our servers for faster personalization on return visits.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or ad targeting.
  • We do not collect passwords, payment information, or personal communications.
  • We do not use your data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Data storage

Behavioral data and page models are stored locally in Chrome's storage API and on our Cloudflare Workers backend. Data is associated with your browser instance, not with a named account, unless you choose to sign in.

Data retention

Local data persists until you uninstall the Extension or clear its storage. Server-side data is retained for the duration of the demo period and deleted thereafter.

Your control

  • You can deactivate any domain at any time from the side panel.
  • You can clear all stored data from the Extension settings.
  • Uninstalling the Extension removes all local data immediately.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, reach out at of1.live.
