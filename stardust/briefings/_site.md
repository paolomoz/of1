# Purpose

of1 is the website for Audience of ONE — an Adobe product for 1:1 web relevance at scale. The site has three jobs: make people feel something (landing), make them feel the inevitability (why), then make them understand how (editorial). Every page drives toward early access or the next page in the reading journey.

# Navigation

- Primary: of1 wordmark (home) | Why now (→ why) | How it works (→ editorial) | See it live (CTA)
- Footer: Audience of ONE | of1.live

# Shared Messaging

- Tagline: The marketing nirvana is real
- Key line: "We made your website pay attention — so every visitor finds exactly what they came for, as if it had always been there."
- Positioning: of1 sells the end of audiences as a concept. It's not better personalization — it's the death of impersonal.

# Content Hierarchy

The landing page is the front door — emotional, manifesto energy, desire before explanation. The why page is the vision — for people who felt something and need to feel the inevitability before they invest in the how. The editorial page is the depth read — for people who believe the vision and want to understand the product. Landing teases the why; why links to editorial; editorial stands alone as a long-form piece.

- The editorial page can reference landing and why concepts but expands them into product narrative
