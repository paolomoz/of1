# Purpose

of1 is the website for Audience of ONE — an Adobe product for 1:1 web relevance at scale. The site has two jobs: make people feel something (landing), then make them understand why (editorial). Every page drives to a single action: request early access.

# Navigation

- Primary: of1 wordmark (home) | How it works (→ editorial) | Request early access (CTA)
- Footer: Audience of ONE | of1.live

# Shared Messaging

- Tagline: The marketing nirvana is real
- Key line: "We made your website pay attention — so every visitor finds exactly what they came for, as if it had always been there."
- Positioning: of1 sells the end of audiences as a concept. It's not better personalization — it's the death of impersonal.

# Content Hierarchy

The landing page is the front door — emotional, manifesto energy, desire before explanation. The editorial page is the depth read — for people who felt something and want to understand. Landing links to editorial; editorial stands alone as a long-form piece.
