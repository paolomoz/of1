How to use the extension

Three steps to your first
generative website experience.

01
Install
Load it into Chrome
Download the extension zip and decompress it. Open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, then click "Load unpacked" and select the folder.
02
Activate
Open the side panel on arco.coffee
Visit arco.coffee, open the of1 side panel and click Activate. The extension begins indexing the site — reading its structure, cataloguing every page and content block. Takes about a minute.
03
Experience
Browse, then click Personalize
Spend a moment on the site — browse products, read a page or two. As your intent becomes clear, return to the homepage and click Personalize. Watch the page reshape itself around you.
